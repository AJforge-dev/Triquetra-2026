AI Forensics

Deepfake & AI Content Detection

Background

The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence has enabled the creation of highly realistic synthetic media such as deepfake videos, AI-generated images, cloned voices, and manipulated digital content. These technologies are increasingly being misused for misinformation, impersonation, financial fraud, and cybercrime.

Objective

Develop a system capable of detecting AI-generated or manipulated media content and assisting law enforcement agencies in verifying authenticity.

Solution Scope
  • Detection of deepfake videos, images, or audio
  • Identification of AI-generated content signatures
  • Metadata and visual frequency spectrum analysis
  • Confidence scoring for media authenticity
  • Real-time or near real-time forensic scanning capability
Evaluation Parameters
  • Detection accuracy - identifying source AI model/website
  • Scalability and inference latency
  • Ease of use for investigative teams
  • Innovation in biometric/artifact detection approach
  • Practical applicability for cyber forensics
Expected Outcome

A production-grade prototype tool that can assist cyber investigators in identifying manipulated or synthetic digital media reliably.

Detect manipulated synthetic media (deepfake video, cloned voice, synthetic images) and extract model fingerprints to aid law enforcement investigations.

OUTCOME:

Real-time media verification and confidence scoring tool.

Trace illicit fund flows across multi-hop blockchain wallets, mixer services, and decentralized exchanges with graph intelligence.

OUTCOME:

Interactive transaction flow visualization with automated wallet risk scoring.

Lightweight on-site scanning tool to extract browser artifacts, deleted caches, event logs, and build cryptographic chain-of-custody reports.

OUTCOME:

Rapid triage utility delivering fast evidence discovery from target hardware.

Canary asset generation engine that extracts real device signatures and coordinates, bypassing standard obfuscations and alerting officers in real time.

OUTCOME:

Tactical alerting dashboard with non-repudiation forensic logging.

100% air-gapped local AI agent for natural language evidence summarization, charge sheet synthesis, and cyber law references.

OUTCOME:

Zero-telemetry local LLM pipeline preserving complete data confidentiality.

Decentralized credentials, automated zero-knowledge campus access, and smart infrastructure security auditing.

OUTCOME:

Tamper-proof verifiable credentials and intrusion detection ecosystem.

EVALUATION PROTOCOL

Judging Criteria

How jury members and technical experts will evaluate your hackathon submissions.

Innovation & Originality

Creativity of the idea and uniqueness of the technical approach in addressing the problem statement.

Technical Implementation

Code quality, system architecture robustness, modularity, and effective utilization of modern technologies.

Completeness of Solution

The project must be a working end-to-end prototype with functional UI and backend, not merely mockups or conceptual slides.

Scalability & Usability

System capability to handle real-world load, accompanied by an intuitive, simple user experience for operators.

Practical Impact

Effectiveness of the software in solving urgent cyber defense, digital evidence preservation, and investigation challenges.

Presentation & Demo

Clarity in articulating the core problem, live interactive software demonstration, and response to jury questions.
